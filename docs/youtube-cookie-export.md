# Export YouTube cookies for Flow

1. Sign in to `youtube.com` in a browser.
2. Use a cookie-export tool you trust to export cookies for YouTube in Netscape `cookies.txt` format. A browser's developer tools can also be used to copy the `Cookie:` header from a signed-in YouTube request.
3. In Flow, open **Settings → YouTube account**, then choose the file or paste the cookie text/header. Flow checks the required cookies and verifies the account before saving them.
4. Delete the exported file or copied header after import. Treat cookies like a password: anyone who gets them may have full access to your Google account. Never send them to another person or paste them into an online converter.

Cookie exports can expire or be invalidated by Google. If Flow reports expired cookies, export a fresh set and import it again.
